Community Guidelines

Last updated October 21, 2026

Orata is for hanging out with friends and finding your people. These guidelines keep it a good place to be. They apply everywhere on Orata: in servers, DMs, group DMs, voice and video, profiles, server names and icons, emoji, stickers and anything you make Orbit say.

Servers can add their own rules on top, and server moderators enforce those. But no server can allow something these guidelines forbid.

Be respectful

  • No harassment or bullying. Don’t target, threaten, intimidate or pile on anyone, and don’t keep contacting someone who has asked you to stop, including with other accounts.
  • No hate. Don’t attack or demean people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability or serious illness.
  • No threats of violence, and don’t celebrate or encourage real-world violence or violent extremist groups.
  • Don’t encourage self-harm or suicide. If you see someone at risk, report it to Orata staff. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Keep everyone safe

  • Never sexualize minors. We have zero tolerance. We terminate accounts and report it to the authorities.
  • No sharing of intimate images without consent, and no threats to share them.
  • Adult content stays in age-restricted spaces that are clearly marked, never where it can surprise people, and never in profile pictures, banners, server icons, emoji or stickers.
  • Don’t share gore or real-world violent content to shock people.
  • Respect privacy. Don’t share someone’s personal information, such as their address, phone number, photos or private messages, without their permission, and never to threaten or expose them (“doxxing”).

No scams, spam or cheating the system

  • No scams or phishing: fake giveaways (“free Nitro”), fake login pages, IP grabbers, malware, or tricking people out of their accounts or money.
  • No spam: flooding channels, mass mentions, raids, spreading invite links around servers, or sending unwanted messages to lots of people.
  • Don’t sell or trade accounts, servers, server tags or badges.
  • No ban dodging. If you’re banned from a server, or your Orata account is locked or terminated, don’t come back with another account.
  • Don’t break Orata. Don’t attack, overload or hack Orata or other people’s accounts. If you find a bug, especially a security one, report it to Orata staff instead of using it.

Be real

  • Don’t impersonate other people, brands or Orata staff. Names like “Orata Staff” or “Orata Mod” are blocked for a reason. Real staff have a staff badge on their profile.
  • Don’t spread harmful misinformation that could lead to real-world harm, such as dangerous health claims or fake emergency alerts.

Follow the law

  • Don’t use Orata to buy, sell or promote illegal goods or services, such as drugs, weapons, stolen accounts or stolen data.
  • Only share content you have the right to share. Don’t post pirated material or other people’s work as your own.

Reporting

  • Something against these guidelines? Right-click the message, the person or the server and choose Report to Orata staff. Reports are private: the person you report isn’t told who reported them.
  • Something against a server’s own rules? Tell that server’s moderators.
  • When something is reported or flagged, staff can look at the conversation or server it’s in to understand what happened. They don’t look at anything that wasn’t reported or flagged (see our Privacy Policy).

What happens if you break them

Orata staff look at reports and at things Orata flags automatically. Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, we may:

  • delete the content;
  • lock the account for a while (from an hour up to 30 days);
  • terminate the account for good;
  • act on a server, up to removing it, if it’s built around breaking these rules.

For the most serious problems, such as anything that endangers a child or someone’s life, we act right away and may contact the authorities. If you think we got it wrong, message Orata staff in the Orata Community server and we’ll take another look.
