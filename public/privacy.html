Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

The short version

  • We collect what Orata needs to work: your account, what you post, and a little technical information to keep spam and ban dodgers out.
  • We don’t sell your information, and Orata has no ads.
  • Orata staff only look at chats when they have to: they see messages, servers and DMs only when they were reported or automatically flagged. Every time they do, it’s written in a staff log.
  • You can delete your messages and your account whenever you like.

This policy explains what information Orata (“we”, “us”) collects, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Orata website, apps and services.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your username, display name, password (we only store a scrambled “hash” of it, never the password itself), date of birth (to check you’re old enough), and your email address if you add one (it’s optional).
  • Profile: your avatar, banner, bio, pronouns, status, profile colours and effects, badges and the server tag you wear.
  • What you post: messages, edits, files, images, voice messages, GIFs, stickers, reactions, polls, and the servers, channels, roles, emoji and invites you create. Also applications you send to join servers, and reports you send to Orata staff.
  • Your connections: friends, friend requests, people you block, the servers you’re in and your DMs.
  • Settings: your look, notification and chat preferences, which we save so they follow you to other devices.

Information we collect automatically

  • Sessions: when you sign in, we keep a record of the session with the name of your browser, so you stay signed in and can see your signed-in devices.
  • A browser ID: Orata stores a random ID in your browser (in a cookie and in local storage). We keep it only as a scrambled hash, at most 8 per account, for up to 180 days. We use it only to stop spam, to limit how many accounts one browser can make, and to find accounts that dodge bans.
  • IP address: we use it to limit abuse, such as too many sign-ups or messages at once. We don’t save it to your account.
  • Activity and status: whether you’re online, what you’re listening to if you share it, and when you last read a channel.

2. How we use it

  • To run Orata: to show your messages to the people they’re meant for, deliver notifications, keep your settings, and let you join servers, voice channels and calls.
  • To keep people safe: Orata automatically checks messages for things like spam bursts, scam and phishing links, mass mentions, invite spam, brand-new accounts messaging lots of people, and many accounts on one browser. Suspicious things are flagged for staff to look at. These checks also look at the text of DMs for scam links. For the “messaging lots of people” check we only count how many people were messaged, not what was said. Server owners can also turn on AutoMod and word filters in their own servers.
  • To talk to you: for example, to email you a code when you sign up with an email address, if email verification is on.
  • To follow the law and respond to valid legal requests.

3. Who can see your information

Other people on Orata

Your profile is visible to other users. A message is visible to everyone who can see the place you posted it: everyone in a DM or group DM, or everyone with access to a server channel. Server owners and moderators can see things in their own server, such as its audit log, who used a bot command, and applications sent to join it.

Orata staff

People with the Orata staff badge help us enforce the Terms and Community Guidelines. Staff don’t browse your messages, servers or DMs. They only look at something when it was reported by a user or flagged by our automatic safety checks. Then they can:

  • Review reports and flags: see messages, accounts and servers that someone reported or that Orata flagged automatically, including the reported message itself.
  • Look into reported or flagged servers: when a server, or messages in it, are reported or flagged, staff can read that server’s channels, messages and member list to understand what happened. They can’t post or change anything while they do.
  • Read reported or flagged DMs: DMs and group DMs are private. Staff can only read one when a message in it was reported by someone in the conversation or flagged automatically (for example, a scam link). They then see that message and the messages around it, and nothing else. Staff have no way to browse other DMs.
  • Look at accounts: see details such as when an account was made, which servers it’s in, other accounts used on the same browser, and its flags and reports.
  • Take action: delete messages, lock or terminate accounts, and act on servers.

Every time a staff member looks inside a server or reads a DM, and every action they take, is recorded in a staff log that other staff can see.

Services we work with

  • Hosting and storage: Orata runs on cloud hosting and database providers that store and process data for us.
  • Email: if email verification is on, we send codes through an email provider.
  • GIFs and stickers (KLIPY): what you type into the GIF and sticker search is sent to KLIPY to find results.
  • Music (Apple): music searches and previews come from Apple Music’s public catalogue. Our server does the searching, so Apple doesn’t get your account details.
  • Link previews and videos: to preview a link, our server visits that website. YouTube videos play in YouTube’s own player, which is covered by Google’s privacy policy.
  • Discord: if a server links a channel with a Discord channel, messages posted in that channel (with your display name and avatar) are copied to Discord, and messages from Discord are copied into Orata. Once on Discord, Discord’s privacy policy applies.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm. We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

4. How long we keep it

  • Messages stay until you or a moderator delete them, or the server or DM they’re in is deleted.
  • Voice messages delete themselves after 24 hours.
  • Browser IDs are kept for up to 180 days after they were last used.
  • Reports, flags and the staff log keep their most recent 1,000 entries. A server’s audit log keeps its latest 500 entries.
  • When you delete your account, we remove your email address, password, profile, friends and settings, and delete the servers you own. Messages you sent in other people’s servers and DMs stay where they are, but now show “Deleted User”.
  • We may keep some information longer if we must for legal reasons or to deal with abuse, such as a record that an account was terminated.

5. Your choices

  • Edit your profile, settings and server tag whenever you like.
  • Delete your own messages, or leave servers and DMs.
  • Block people so they can’t message you.
  • Use Orata without an email address.
  • Delete your account in User Settings → My Account.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or for anything else about your privacy, contact us (see below). Depending on where you live, you may have more rights by law, and we’ll honour them.

6. Children

Orata is not for children under 13 (or older, where the law requires it). If we learn that an account belongs to someone too young, we will delete it.

7. Security

We protect your information with measures such as hashed passwords, private session tokens and HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don’t use anywhere else.

8. Changes to this policy

When we update this policy, we’ll change the date at the top, and for big changes we’ll let you know in Orata.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Message Orata staff in the Orata Community server, or use Report to Orata staff in the app.
